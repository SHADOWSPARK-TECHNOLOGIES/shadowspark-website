## Partner Qualification Checklist

- [ ] Clear use-case with measurable success metric.
- [ ] Technical integration target identified (API, SDK, marketplace listing).
- [ ] At least one committed engineer on the partner side.
- [ ] Legal/security review path agreed.
- [ ] Pilot start date, check-in cadence, and exit criteria defined.
- [ ] Mutual marketing/PR agreement on public case study.

## Follow-up Sequence

1. Day 0: Send tailored outreach email.
2. Day 3: LinkedIn connection + short voice note.
3. Day 7: Follow-up with one relevant product link (/products or /infrastructure-trust).
4. Day 14: Send calendar link and pilot one-pager.
5. Day 21: Close or nurture into community/newsletter.
